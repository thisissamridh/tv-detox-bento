# Apps safe to turn off on a Sony BRAVIA with Google TV (tested on a 2023 KD-43X75L, Google TV 14).
# Turned off, not deleted: undo.sh turns them all back on. Lines starting with # are skipped, so
# put a # in front of anything you want to keep.
#
#   sh tune.sh <TV_IP> debloat/sony-bravia.txt
